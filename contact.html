FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What boards does Kaizen Academy cover? +

Kaizen Academy offers coaching for all four major boards: SSC (Maharashtra State Board), ICSE, CBSE, and IGCSE (Cambridge International). We have dedicated batches structured around board-specific requirements.

What are the Sarthi, Apex and Ikigai batches? +

These are Kaizen Academy's three signature academic batches. Sarthi is for grades 9–10 SSC students, Apex is for grades 9–10 ICSE/CBSE/IGCSE students, and Ikigai is for grades 7–8 across all boards.

Can my child join both academic and activity programmes? +

Absolutely! Many students attend both. Our academic and activity schedules are designed to complement each other with no conflicts. Activity classes run on separate slots — often weekends or early evenings.

Is Mind Vista only for 10th or 12th grade students? +

Mind Vista counselling is available to students at any stage of their academic journey — from early planning in grade 9 to last-minute applications for university. We recommend starting early for the best outcomes.

What kind of certificates does Saksham offer? +

Saksham is affiliated with the Government of India's skill development initiative and issues nationally recognised certificates aligned with the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF). These are valid across India and respected by employers.

How do I enrol or book a consultation? +

Simply fill in the inquiry form on this page, call us at +91 99999 99999, or WhatsApp us. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours to guide you through the enrolment or consultation process.
