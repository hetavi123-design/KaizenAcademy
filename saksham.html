About Saksham

Skills That Open Doors

Saksham — meaning "capable" in Sanskrit — is our promise to every student and professional who walks through our doors. We are dedicated to equipping individuals with practical, industry-relevant skills that lead to real employment opportunities.

As a government-affiliated center, Saksham's training programmes adhere to nationally defined skill standards. Graduates receive certifications that are valid across India and recognised by employers in both the public and private sectors.
